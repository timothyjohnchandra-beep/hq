# Timothy HQ – rules for Claude

Personal daily app for Timothy (Executive Pastor, GPKdI BCC; director of PT Citra Warna Mulia Tama and PT Peregrine Indo Chemica). He is the only user.

## How we work
1. Simplest setup that works. Do the building yourself; give Timothy numbered, phone-friendly steps only when he must act.
2. Mockup first for anything big (new tab, redesign); wait for his pick. "Build it" = build with sensible defaults, ask afterwards.
3. Never replace the design. Improve on top of it (Apple Notes feel: white sheets, grouped lists, notes yellow #ffd60a, Inter).
4. Nothing fails silently: every sync, calendar or save failure shows a plain message saying why.
5. Never lose history: rows are marked `void`, not deleted. The script keeps `_bak_<tab>` (10 min) and `_day_<tab>` (daily) copies.
6. Check GitHub, Drive, Sheets and connectors yourself before asking Timothy to do something.
7. Copy in plain, warm, human English. No buzzwords.
8. Save his decisions to memory.

## Architecture
- `index.html`: the whole app (plain HTML/CSS/JS, no build). Tabs: Today, Notes, Money, People, Library, Settings. Library lists the `books` tab (id = Drive file id; category = 01–14 code matching Claude/Library folders; minutes, summary, usefulFor, keyPoints split by " | ") grouped by category (categories listed A–Z by name after Continue reading; the Sheet turns "01" into 1, so `ensure()` pads single digits — never compare raw category values), with one Read button and a More menu (Open in Drive, Progress & highlights, Add to a paper, Move to category = patch category; Claude's summaries task moves the Drive file to match), with Open Library covers cached per device, tap-to-expand details and search. Library › "Add a PDF or EPUB from this device" posts `addbook` (≤30 MB) → saved in Claude/Library/<category by title> and opened. `reader.js` (loaded on demand, epub.js + JSZip from jsDelivr) is the EPUB reader: Apple Books layout (close top-left, bookmark top-right, back bottom-left, ⋯ menu bottom-right, progress bar), themes Original/Quiet/Paper/Bold/Calm/Focus/Night, fonts, size, line/letter spacing, margins, justify, pages or scroll, contents (links resolved to the real spine section, then the anchor's exact spot — `goHref`), search, go to page/%, highlights (5 colours, underline) and notes, bookmarks, study cards, vocabulary (Kindle), reading ruler, read aloud, copy with source, translate, Ask Claude / Story so far (opens Claude), send highlights to Notes, reading goals with streak (reading_log tab), time left in chapter/book. Books are cached on the device in Cache API `hq-books` (sw.js must not delete it). Highlights tab: kind = highlight|bookmark|word, cfi, chapter, style (u = underline). Money is a Cashbook-style ledger (books, cash in/out, category, payment mode, running balance, filters, report with CSV/print); Today's order (Timothy, 23 Nov 2026): At a glance (with Today's priorities = `briefs` section `priority`, from the daily Chief of Staff brief) → Verse of the day → News brief → Email (Key emails = `briefs` section `keymail` first, then Read for you, then the inbox) → the rest. Navigation: HQ always opens on Today; coming back after 2+ min reloads data from the Sheet; after 10+ min away it returns to Today; back always steps toward Today (guard re-armed on every tap); the Notes tab always opens the Notes main list. Today shows a Cashbook card, the Email section ("Read for you": Claude's morning summaries of watched senders from `briefs` rows with section `mail`, tag Samuel|Stockbit|YLG, then the unread inbox with gists), the News brief card (latest date in the `briefs` tab: section headline|attention|id|world, n, tag, title, summary, link) and a "For Claude" list. "With Claude" (after News brief) = our unfinished projects together: tasks with `for=together` (or older rows whose notes start "Claude to-do"); they are kept out of the day's task lists and Overdue. Whenever Claude finishes or starts a project with Timothy in chat, add or close its row (status done) in the tasks tab; the daily "Morning brief + Chief of Staff" scheduled task also reviews them (closes rows only on clear evidence from memory's running list, Claude Session Notes or the data, and flags rows stuck 7+ days). People › "From Google Contacts" lists contacts not yet in People (search, Add brings name, number, birthday); once a day after loading, HQ quietly fills missing phones/birthdays in People from Google Contacts (cached per device in `hq-contacts`). Verse of the day: YouVersion's og:description now starts with the reference, so `verse_` splits it off. The Claude button (floating on phones, in the sidebar on wide screens) saves tasks/notes with `for=claude`, `type=task|note` in the tasks tab and can open claude.ai/new prefilled.
- `sw.js`: network first, cached copy offline; never caches Google calls. Bump `CACHE` when shell files change.
- `manifest.webmanifest` + `icons/`: installable on iPhone, iPad, Android, Mac. Icon (Timothy's pick, 22 Nov 2026): "TJC" in thin Montserrat (weight 250) with a thin line under it, black #111 on warm white #faf8f0. Montserrat is for the icon only; the app text stays Inter.
- `apps-script.gs`: the only API. Deployed as a Web app by Timothy (Execute as me, Anyone). GET `?action=all|read|calendar|brief|note|verse|contacts|book|img|ping&token=` (img = a Drive picture as base64, used for imported notes because iPhone Safari blocks drive.google.com pictures; the app caches them in Cache API `hq-imgs`) (book = base64 EPUB, max 40 MB); POST `{token, action: save|note|addEvent|attach|patch|addbook}` (patch = change cells of one row by id; the reader uses it so it never rewrites the whole books tab). Travel times come from Maps in the script, from home or the previous event; leave-by = start − drive − 10 min.
- Data: Google Sheet "HQ Data" (tabs: tasks, people, notes_index, places, habits, books, highlights, cashbooks, cash_entries, briefs, reading_log, meetings). Note bodies are HTML files in Drive "Claude/HQ Notes" (attachments in "Claude/HQ Notes/Attachments"). Everything Claude creates in Drive goes inside the "Claude" folder. All values stored as text; dates yyyy-mm-dd.
- The bridge has TWO active Web app deployments; Timothy's phone uses the older one (first made 23 Nov 2026). Every bridge update: give BOTH a New version, then check HQ › Settings shows the new SCRIPT_VERSION.
- The script URL and token live only in the app's Settings (localStorage on each device). Without them the app shows sample data.
- Claude in chat reads and updates the Sheet through the Google Sheets connector.

## Library sorting (bridge script)
`sortNewBooks` runs hourly (and from Library's "Check Drive now", GET action=sortbooks): every new EPUB anywhere in Drive goes to Claude/Library/<category> by title keywords and is added to the books tab (format, added). Under [Teaching] = copy (tracked so it copies once); elsewhere = move. PDFs are only picked up inside Library or Papers folders. Claude/Papers/<paper title>/ holds sources for a paper: EPUBs, PDFs or Drive shortcuts to Library books stay there and get `paper` (semicolon list) in the books tab; Library shows a "Paper: …" group and "Add to a paper" sets the same field. The reader opens PDFs with pdf.js (real page numbers, text selection, highlights stored as cfi `pdf:N`, chapter `p. N`). Unclear titles land in "00 New – to sort" for Claude to place by hand. Categories: 01 OT, 02 NT, 03 Theology, 04 Holy Spirit/Pentecost/Revival, 05 Discipleship & Spiritual Formation, 06 Church & Pastoral Leadership, 07 Counseling, 08 Apologetics, 09 Leadership & Business, 10 Personal Growth, 11 Money & Giving, 12 Marriage/Family/Youth, 13 Fiction, 14 History/Economics, 99 Duplicates & files to check.

## New book routine (agreed with Timothy, 23 Nov 2026)
1. Timothy drops an EPUB anywhere in Drive (PDFs: inside a Library or Papers folder).
2. Within the hour the bridge's `sortNewBooks` files it into Claude/Library/<category> by title keywords and adds a books row; unclear titles go to "00 New – to sort".
3. The scheduled task "Library: book summaries" (every 3 h while there is a backlog, then daily 5:12 a.m.) fixes the category of 00/wrong books (moves the file to the matching Library folder), writes summary, usefulFor and keyPoints in Claude's own words, and reports duplicates and books it couldn't verify.
4. Timothy can move any book with Library › More › Move to category; new category names: he tells Claude, who adds them to LIBCATS/CATCOL, the bridge's category list and a Drive folder.

## Meetings (Fireflies → Drive)
`exportFireflies` in the bridge script (every 3 days at 4 a.m. via `setupMeetingsTrigger`, or GET action=meetings) reads Fireflies' GraphQL API with the key in Script properties `FIREFLIES_KEY`, saves each transcribed meeting as a Google Doc (summary, action items, notes, transcript with times) in Claude/Meetings/<year>/, and adds a row to the `meetings` tab. With `FIREFLIES_DELETE=yes` it then deletes the meeting from Fireflies to free minutes (only after the Doc exists). Fireflies' free plan never transcribes recordings past the minute limit ("skipped"); those have no text to export. An HQ Meetings view is still to be built (mockup first).

## Notes import and verse search
`importNotes` (bridge, one-time, re-runnable in 4½-min rounds) brings the Apple Notes export (Drive "iCloud", folders kept) and Google Keep export (Drive "Google/Notes", sorted into subfolders by `KEEP_SORT`/`KEEP_WORDS`) into HQ Notes as `md-<fileId>` rows with tag `imported`. Notes matching `SENSITIVE`/long digit runs are left out (Keep ones moved to "Private – not in HQ"). `parseRefs` (Indonesian TB + English book names) fills the `refs` column (e.g. `JHN.3.16-18`); `indexVerses` backfills older notes. In Notes, searching a reference ("yoh 3:16", "Filipi") shows the verse panel: this verse / same chapter / same book, Summarise with Claude, Start a new sermon.

## Sermon map
View `sermons` (from Notes › Sermon map and Today › Quick links). Rows: `sermons` tab (id, noteId, title, date, place, context, series, refs, themes, theology, points " | ", bigIdea, preacher = "me" or name, docLink, void, updated, checked); refs separated by spaces or "; "; a card with no HQ note opens its docLink. Sermon Docs live in Drive Claude/Sermons/<01–09 context folder> (00 To check = unsure); since 24 Nov 2026 Gemini indexes them (Timothy's choice): the bridge's `indexSermonsGemini` (hourly via `setupGeminiSermons`; key in Script property `GEMINI_KEY` set with `setGeminiKey`, tries the Lite models first (about 1 s each), then full Flash/Hydrogen, resting any that are out of free quota for a day, or `GEMINI_MODEL` to force one; every call carries `X-Server-Timeout` (≤45 s) because a busy Gemini held single requests for 2–6 minutes and UrlFetchApp has no timeout; a run ends by ~290 s, ≤25 Docs, timed lines in the Execution log; on 24 Nov 2026 the key listed no Hydrogen model, newest was gemini-3.8-flash) finds new sermon Docs, has Gemini fill the row (refs and context checked, never invented), records non-sermons in `sermon_moves` as folder `skip`, then runs `sortSermons`; the Claude scheduled task "Sermon Docs: index and sort" is paused. Claude's Drive connector cannot move files, so `sortSermons` (hourly via `setupSermonTrigger`, or GET action=sortsermons) moves each Doc to its folder from the row's context (preacher ≠ me → 09) and from the `sermon_moves` tab (id, folder, title, added) for briefs/themes (08) and unclear Docs (00) that get no row; notes in folders matching /sermon|khotbah|preach|teaching|pengajaran/ without a row still show (title, date, verses). Filters: Mine/Others/Both, Place, Context, Theme, Theology, Year, Book (Bible map), search or verse. "Summarise with Claude" sends the filtered list.

## Who does what: Gemini, Claude, Telegram (agreed 26 Nov 2026)
- **Gemini = the night shift inside the bridge script.** Free, runs on timers, no chat. It does fixed, repeatable jobs on data already in HQ: news brief (5:20 a.m.), sermon indexing (hourly), and the Telegram bot's thinking (sorting messages into tasks/notes/receipts, answering questions from HQ data, group summaries, to-dos, follow-ups). Its behaviour lives in the code; nobody instructs Gemini in a chat.
- **Claude = judgment and building.** Morning brief + Chief of Staff priorities and key emails (scheduled task), Library summaries, sermon and teaching prep, research, writing, decks, and every change to HQ, the bridge and the bot. Claude reads Gmail/Drive/Calendar through connectors and is the only one who edits the bridge.

"Claude, …" messages land on HQ's For Claude list; Claude works through that list when Timothy opens a chat ("do my For Claude list") or in the nightly review.
- **Where Timothy gives instructions:** to build or change anything → here (Claude, "Personal HQ App" project). Quick capture or a question on the go → Telegram. Never paste code from the Gemini app or another chat into the bridge: on 26 Nov 2026 a pasted Gemini rewrite had blanked the Sheet ID (every timer failed for two days) and switched the bot to a webhook that dropped messages. GitHub is the master copy; Claude pastes it.

## Telegram bot (rebuilt 26 Nov 2026)
`telegram-news.gs` is a second file in the bridge's Apps Script project. Script properties: `GEMINI_KEY`, `TELEGRAM_TOKEN`; `TELEGRAM_CHAT` (Timothy's private chat = his user id) is set by the first /start; `tgOffset`, `tgBriefSent`, `tgFollowSent`, `tgRecapSent`, `tgGroups` (JSON of allowed groups with lastSummaryTs). **Polling only** (`pollTelegram` every minute, getUpdates; for 3 min after Timothy's last private message it stays awake ~45 s with long polling (`tgAwake`), so replies take seconds; idle runs stay ~1 s because a free Google account gets ~90 min of timer run time a day): a webhook to an Apps Script web app gets a redirect, so Telegram retries/drops, and it runs the deployed version, not the saved code. `setupTelegramAndNews` deletes any webhook and makes four triggers: `newsGemini` (daily ~5:20), `pollTelegram` (1 min), `pushBrief` (15 min, 6–10 a.m.), `tgHourly` (9 a.m. follow-ups, 9 p.m. recap). `telegramStatus` logs webhook state.
- **Private chat** (Timothy only): text, voice, forwarded message, receipt photo, PDF → Gemini: task → tasks tab; "Claude, …" → `for=claude`; idea/note → a real HQ note in Notes folder `Telegram` (`tgSaveCapture_`, tag telegram, Undo voids the notes_index row); "Note: …" / "Catatan: …" / "/note …" skips Gemini and saves the text word for word (first line = title, "- " lines = bullets) (Timothy, 27 Nov 2026: notes must land in Notes, not For Claude); receipt → cash_entries (first cashbook, or a new "Personal" book); question → answer from calendar/tasks/group to-dos/sermons/notes/inbox. Buttons: Done, Tomorrow, world, Undo. Commands /today /tasks /news /groups /recap /help.
- **Groups**: the bot stays only where Timothy added it or is a member (otherwise leaveChat). Needs BotFather privacy **disabled** to see normal messages. Every message is logged in tab `tg_log` (id, chat, group, date, time, ts, from, text, kind, file; voice notes keep file_id and are transcribed at summary time, ≤6 per summary). /summary → summary, decisions, to-dos, open questions in bullets, posted in the group, saved as an HQ note in folder `Telegram/<group>` (tag telegram), to-dos to tab `tg_todos` (id, chat, group, task, owner, due, status, created, updated, nudged, source); Timothy's own to-dos also become tasks rows (source `hq:<taskId>`, ticked together). /todo, /todo <text>, /done N, ✅ buttons (anyone in the group). 9 a.m.: follow-up post per group for due/overdue to-dos (and undated ones older than 3 days), each at most every 2 days, plus a digest to Timothy. 9 p.m.: groups with ≥5 new messages get a private recap to Timothy (same note + to-dos). tg_log/tg_todos are not in TABS (the app doesn't load them).
- **Day notes (Timothy, 27 Nov 2026)**: `tgDailyNotes_` (hourly from `tgHourly`; also /notes in private chat and every /summary) writes ONE note per chat per day, id `tgd-<chat>-<yyyymmdd>`, title "<chat> – Fri 27 Nov 2026", folder `Telegram/<chat>`; Timothy's private chat with the bot is logged in tg_log as group `TJC PA` (voice notes keep Gemini's transcript) and gets its own day note. Note = summary, decisions, to-dos, open questions, then the full chat. Rebuilt only when the message count changed (`tgDaily`); just after midnight yesterday's note is finished. Group voice notes are transcribed once and the words saved into tg_log. /summary and the 9 p.m. recap no longer make separate notes.

## Claude session notes (built 27 Nov 2026)
At the end of every working session with Timothy (voice or text), Claude writes a Google Doc into Drive "Claude/Claude Session Notes", titled "yyyy-mm-dd – <topic>", with headings: Minutes (numbered), Artifacts (each a link), To-do / Follow-ups ("- [ ]" items, or plain bullets under that heading). The bridge's `syncSessionNotes` (hourly via `setupSessionNotes`, or GET action=sessions) turns each Doc into an HQ note `cs-<fileId>` in Notes folder "Claude Sessions" (tag claude): to-dos become tickable checklist items, links open on tap. When Claude adds to a Doc later, the note is rebuilt and items Timothy ticked stay ticked (Script property `csDone` = file id → last change). Ticks live in HQ, not in the Doc.
